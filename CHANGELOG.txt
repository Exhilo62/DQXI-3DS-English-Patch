DQXI 3DS English patch - changelog
==================================
  * RECOMMENDED: always make a NEW full patched .cia / .3ds from the newest version and install that.
    Update (Luma) folders are turned off for now (they cannot carry the code edits and caused battle crashes on some setups).
    Because patches are frequent, new installs give the best results. Sorry for the frequent updates - they are needed as the
    translation keeps improving. Update folders will return in a future patch once the game is more stable.
    If you still have an old update folder on your SD card, delete it (SD:/luma/titles/0004000000199200).

0.1.17
  * Patcher: it now checks the finished .cia/.3ds (checksums and every patched file) and deletes it if something is wrong; it says clearly when the file is not the Japanese game or is already patched; it warns when the drive is FAT32 and cannot hold the 4 GB .3ds; and it writes a small "patch log" next to the output. New buttons "Copy diagnostics" and "Report a problem..." put a short, anonymous summary on your clipboard for bug reports. The window now shows real progress with the time left, remembers your last game file and output folder, and has an "Open output folder" button for when it finishes. The "Done" box now gives short install steps (delete the old SD:/luma folder first, install the .cia with FBI) and tells you where to find the version stamp on the title screen.
  * The patcher's "Create update folder" button is turned off for now (see above); make a full .cia / .3ds.
  * The title screen now shows "English patch v0.1.17" under the logo, so screenshots and bug reports show which version you are on. The title menu entry "Resume" is now "Susp Log" (the Suspend Log, same wording as the save screens).
  * More official names (checked against the official English script): Humphrey is now "Vince", Ketos is "Cetacea", Mount Hinonogi/Hinonogi Volcano is "Mount Huji", the Golden Plague is the "Gold Fever", Ancestral Forest is "First Forest", Mysterious Forge is "Fun-Size Forge", Sacred Lambda is "Arboria", Nautica Village is "Lonalulu", Jiego is "Don Rodrigo", Bandelfon is "Zwaardsrust", Nelsen is "Drustan" and Uranos is "Morcant" (dialogue, quests, books, map labels).
  * Even more official names: Faris Cup is the "Sand National", Diana is "Akia", Duruda (village) is "Angri-La", Kainui Yuki is "Kainoa", Roche is "Erdwin", Lord Lowe is "Lord Robert", Puchalao Village is "Phnom Nonh", the Sea Man Contest is "Signor Universo", the witch Lizlet is "Krystalinda", and Lord/General Hendrik and Jasper are "Sir Hendrik" and "Sir Jasper".
  * Town names corrected to the official ones (they were rotated): the canal town with the Signor Universo contest is "Gondolia" (was shown as Puerto Valor), the arena town is "Octagonia" (was Gondolia) and the Spanish-style port town of Don Rodrigo is "Puerto Valor" (was Soltico). Map labels for the desert kingdom now read Gallopolis.
  * More official names: Goliath is "Norberto", Maya is "Mia" (Erik's sister), Lyla/Laila is "Lala", Grayg is "Hendrik", Rahadio is "Doge Rotondo", Nizelfa is "Calasmos", the undersea kingdom Muurea is "Nautica", Napugana Jungle is "Manglegrove", Hotto Village is "Hotto", Bakurava Dunes are the "Celestial Sands", and the quest characters Apollo, Seidon and Delgins are "Hakim", "Valentino" and "Xero".
  * Checked story and NPC lines against the official script: the inn is "Warrior's Rest Inn", Lupus is "Noah", the Last Fortress is the "Last Bastion", Dharma Temple is "Alltrades Abbey", Bird Valley is the "Eerie Eyrie", "Samaday" now reads Gallopolis, and doubled titles ("Lady Queen Frysabel", "Queen Queen Marina") are fixed.
  * Fixed text that scrolled or overlapped after the name changes: 24 NPC and party-chat lines that had grown to four lines are tightened to three (the Heliodor tourist's book chat no longer overlaps the next message), the overhead bubbles are re-fitted (13 bubbles, including "Hendrik's valor, Jasper's wits..."), 23 quest-log entries that had a too-long line are re-wrapped, and four key item names are shortened to fit the list ("Gallopolis Letter", "Hotto's Treasure", "Drustan's Arms", "Noah's Memo"). "Great Training Hall" is now the "Field of Discipline".
  * Reported by testers and fixed: the lone opening quote mark that appeared at the start of NPC lines is gone; the Adventure Log world town "Garai Town" is "Galenholm" and the bard Garai is "Galen"; the Labyrinth of Time menu ("Motivation", "Items Obtained", "End Search") and the hat-shop header ("Cost") no longer overflow; the "You can now go to ... in the Adventure Log world of ..." message is re-broken; short quest request texts shown in the narrow bottom-screen quest window are re-wrapped so words no longer split; and the Hotto bath poem fits its box.
  * Item and equipment names now match the official English names (checked against the dragon-quest.org item pages by Japanese name): about 490 names, for example "Strong Medicine", "Drasilian Shilling", "Crimsonite", "Erik's Dagger", "Serena's Wand", "Sylvando's Sword", "Gemma's Charm", the Goomerang and Gooreatsword families, and "Lorelei's Harp" (was Mermaid Harp). Names are kept short enough for the item list; where the official name is too long a close short form is used. The same names are used in quests, dialogue and shops.
  * The party menu message for swapping equipment between two characters was still Japanese; it now reads "<name> swapped <A>'s <item> with <B>'s <item>."
  * Leftover names fixed: Master/Lord Rodrigo is "Don Rodrigo", Soltiana Coast is "Costa Valor" (text and map labels), and "Master Pang" is "Grand Master Pang".
  * Fixes: victory line "gains ... experience points!" no longer runs off the box; Magic menu spell descriptions and the Tockle (StreetPass) menu no longer overflow; Link move names fit the bottom screen; "Queen's Letter" no longer overlaps in the key item list.
  * Faris's Growth Journal: the last diary page (the entry about Faris listening to the townsfolk) was still Japanese; it is translated now.
  * Link (combo) menu in battle: long move names (Super Rampage Circle, Ice-Thunder Circle, Beast Mode) and the effect and "Needs the Zone" descriptions no longer overlap or get cut off.
  * Gemma's speech at the start ("We, the people of Cobblestone, live with the spirits of the earth...") now says "the Spirit of the Land", like the rest of the game.
  * "Private Chambers of the King and Queen" (location title) is now "Royal Bedchamber" and the map label "Prince's Chambers" is "Prince's Room", so neither is cut off.

    especially a code.ips, override the new install, so text stays untranslated (the battle Plan menu

  * Names: "Darkspawn" (was Devil Child), "Luminary" (was Hero), the Sword of Light, "Derk" (was Deku),
    "Cobblestone Tor" and the Spirit of the Land, "Teo", and the coming-of-age (and all other) rites and rituals are "ceremony".
  * Fit fixes: Title List, in-game save menu place names, camp menu ("Records"), Stats screen, weapon, armor and monster
    category names, a pixel re-check that re-wrapped every dialogue line that ran out of the text box.
  * The battle Plan menu's last row reads "Party" in a full patched .cia / .3ds (update folders cannot change it).
  * The Yocchi are now "Tockles" and Yocchi Village is "Tickington", the official English names (the StreetPass feature is "Tockle-tch").
